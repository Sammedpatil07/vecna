Help - Vecna

Resetting Your Password:

If you’re having trouble logging in, you can reset your password by clicking the "Forgot Password" option on the login screen. Follow the instructions sent to your email to securely reset your password.

Managing Privacy Settings:

You can customize your privacy settings by adjusting who can see your posts, profile, and personal information. Visit the Privacy section in the settings menu to make these changes.

Reporting a Problem:

If you encounter inappropriate content, bugs, or other issues, use the "Report a Problem" feature found in your settings. Provide as many details as possible to help us address the issue promptly.

Need Further Assistance? If your question isn’t answered here, check back for future updates to our Help Center. We’re constantly working to improve your experience on Vecna.

Thank you for being part of the Vecna community!
